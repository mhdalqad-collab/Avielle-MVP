## Original home-only dashboard illustration

The layered interface in `components/home-dashboard-story.tsx` is an original CSS illustration of Avielle's wardrobe design. It reuses the existing photograph above in decorative panels; CSS perspective, transforms and soft shadows provide depth. No external model, new texture, video, WebGL renderer or animation library is used.

The preview is labelled **Dashboard design preview** and **Illustrative layout · your activity appears after sign-in**. It does not represent a rental listing or a live account and contains no fictional balances, metrics, bookings or availability. Actual inventory continues to use lender-uploaded photographs only.

The [user-provided motion reference](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/to8pipP0rBU) informed the scroll-chapter and layered-interface treatment. Its footage and product assets are not embedded or copied into Avielle.
