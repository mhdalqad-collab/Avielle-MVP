## Original home-page 3D visual

The ivory dress, pleats, straps and metallic hanger are original parametric geometry in `components/home-fashion-renderer.ts`, rendered with native WebGL. No external model, texture, video or animation library is used. This is explicitly labelled editorial art, not an available rental garment. The existing photograph is the progressive-loading and unsupported-WebGL fallback.

The [user-provided reference](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/to8pipP0rBU) informed the floating-object and scroll-chapter motion. Its footage and product assets are not embedded or copied into Avielle.
